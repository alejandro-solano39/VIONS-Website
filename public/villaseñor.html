Villaseñor

International Booking Dj

Villaseñor is one of the most active and representative figures within the Mexican electronic scene. DJ, producer, promoter and label manager (Vigor Trax), recognized for being the first resident of the PERVERT collective. With more than 14 years behind the decks, his impeccable technique and energetic groove-laden productions have led him to position himself among the most important names in the national underground scene. His eclectic sets are pure percussive energy and full of rhythm, loaded with dark bass lines that travel seamlessly between genres.

He has performed at the most prestigious clubs and festivals in Mexico, such as Ceremonia, BoilerRoom, Funk Club, Bar Américas and Hardpop, among others. He has also played at the most important parties and clubs in cities such as Berlin, Amsterdam, Barcelona, ​​New York, San Francisco, Los Angeles, Vancouver, Buenos Aires, Sao Paulo, Santiago, Medellín, Bogotá, San José and more. His music has resonated on the best stages internationally, bringing the Mexican underground sound to new audiences around the world.



At VIONS, we celebrate Villaseñor’s vision of transforming the sound space into a unique and unforgettable experience. His passion for music and his ability to emotionally connect with the audience are a reflection of the innovative spirit we seek to promote.
