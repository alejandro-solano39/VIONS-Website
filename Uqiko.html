Uqiko

Digital Artist; Uqiko

Since 2022

At VIONS, we are dedicated to creating digital marketing that goes beyond the conventional, and our designer Uqiko embodies this philosophy perfectly. Uqiko is a distinguished multidisciplinary artist with a deep affinity for digital art and electronic music. As part of the VIONS team, her work is characterized by the innovative fusion of 3D design, generative design, animations, and artificial intelligence. Aesthetically, Uqiko explores a vibrant palette of contrasting and solid colors, while maintaining an organic and procedural touch in her creations.

Her skills encompass the creation of 3D mockups for galleries, designing album covers for artists, and crafting flyers for events. Uqiko has showcased her talent in significant live audiovisual exhibitions, including performances at the National Center for the Arts (Cenart) and the Digital Culture Center.

In her portfolio, you can explore the impeccable details Uqiko has crafted in her most outstanding works. Each piece reflects the unique vision and innovative spirit that defines VIONS, ensuring a truly captivating experience.
