Sergio

Digital Artist; Sergio

Since 2021

At VIONS, we are committed to bringing marketing and design into a new era, and Sergio is an essential part of this mission. Originally from Mexico City, Sergio is a graphic designer and digital artist who has masterfully combined electronic music with visual art. Since he began his career as a digital artist in 2020, he has focused on creating audio-reactive 3D and audiovisual pieces, as well as developing branding solutions that capture the essence and spirit of each project.

Sergio shares VIONS vision of challenging established boundaries in digital marketing, using his ability to transform musical vibes into dynamic art and futuristic branding. Each of his projects reflects a unique interplay between sound, movement and identity design, achieving immersive visual experiences that deeply connect with audiences. His focus on innovation and passion for creating visual concepts that fuse art and branding make his work stand out in a saturated environment.

As part of the VIONS team, Sergio joins us in our quest to deliver marketing and branding solutions that not only meet expectations, but exceed them with creativity and cuttingedge technology
